The hypothesis testing framework used in frequentist inference described here 
([Frequentist Inference](Inference.md#frequentist-inference)) is pretty standardize. Although in practice this leads to a lot of misuse in statistical analysis, it is still helpful to have a backlog of pre-define tests and a common process.
- For different use-cases there are different hypothesis tests. More on that here: [Different Types of Hypothesis Tests](Different%20Types%20of%20Hypothesis%20Tests.md).
- To perform hypothesis tests we adhere to the [hypothesis testing process](Hypothesis%20Testing%20Process.md).
- And to evaluate hypothesis tests we use the [metrics of hypothesis tests](Metrics%20of%20Hypothesis%20Tests.md).
- An important part of performing hypothesis tests, especially in the case of [clinical trails](Clinical%20Trails.md), is [sample size calculation](Sample%20Size%20Calculation.md).

Here is an example of how to do hypothesis testing in Python: [Hypothesis Testing in Python](Hypothesis%20Testing%20in%20Python.md).
